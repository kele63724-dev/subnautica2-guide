## Enameled Glass (Key Mid-Game Material)

**Creature Enamel** is required for **Enameled Glass**.

| Location | ~300m SE of Overgrown Ruins, depth 200m+ |
|----------|------|
| **Appearance** | White bone-like protrusions on black mineral deposits |
| **Harvest Tool** | **Sonic Resonator** required to break |
| **Locating** | Build a **Scanner Station** in the biome for precise targeting |

Enameled Glass is core to Tadpole depth modules, Impact Armor, and Scout Ray chassis.
