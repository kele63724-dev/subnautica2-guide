# Creatures

Early Access features **88 species** (41 fauna + 47 flora). Below are the most important ones.

![Creature Scale Comparison](/images/creatures/creature-scale.svg)

---

## S-Class Threats — Leviathans

### Collector Leviathan

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Length** | 50m+ |
| **Class** | Leviathan (Cephalopod) |
| **Location** | Sparse Plains, Outer Bounds |
| **Attacks** | 4 tentacles — grab/crush/swat/psi-wave |
| **AI** | Real-time dynamic, not scripted patrol |

**The only aggressive Leviathan at EA launch. Cannot be killed — evade only.**

**Tactics**:
- 🛡️ Create **45,000 units of distance** to trigger despawn
- 🛡️ **Decoy Flares** temporarily distract it
- 🛡️ Tadpole **Cavitation Silencer** reduces detection risk
- ❌ Don't try to fight — there's no winning

---

### Shiver Leviathan

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Location** | The Void |
| **Traits** | Sexually dimorphic (females larger), pack hunters |
| **Depth** | ~5,000m |

Void-exclusive. Packs hunt together — the absolute barrier at the map edge.

---

### Deepwing Brooder

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Class** | Leviathan (Arthropod) |
| **Behavior** | Filter-feeder, passive |
| **Location** | Near-surface waters |

Large harmless filter-feeder. "Carpet-bombs" eggs over the seafloor. Safe to observe.

---

### Great Jaw (Clamthulu)

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Type** | Trap Leviathan |
| **Location** | Tufa Towers |
| **Attack** | Tripwire tentacles → jaw closure → stomach acid |

Giant stationary clam. Tentacles laid out — one touch triggers the trap.

**Tactic**: If you see tentacle-like structures on the ground, go around.

## A-Class Threats

### Needler Mango

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Location** | Overgrown Ruins (Axum Observatory area) |
| **Attack** | Long-range spike projectiles |

"Mango" class fauna. Patrols alien ruins, attacks from range. Dangerous in numbers.

**Tactic**: Keep distance, use cover. Tadpole Impact Armor reduces damage.

## B-Class Threats

### Hoverthorn / Black Hoverthorn

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Location** | Coral Gardens, mid-water |
| **Attack** | Close-range charging |

Medium fish. Aggressive when approached.

## Small Fauna & Useful Species

### Moon Fish Family

| Name | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Halfmoon** | Basic food, abundant near Lifepod |
| **Bluemoon** | Food, schools with Halfmoon |
| **Harvestmoon** | Food, key ingredient for **Threemoon Temaki** |

**Threemoon Temaki** = Halfmoon + Bluemoon + Harvestmoon + Fibrous Pulp. Best early-game food.

---

### Water Slugs

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Location** | Seabed everywhere |
| **Trait** | Blue bioluminescence, easy to find at night |
| **Use** | **Primary water source** — 1 slug + salt = Isotonic Water (+40 H₂O) |

**Flash Slug** is also a Water Slug variant, used in Isotonic Water crafting.
