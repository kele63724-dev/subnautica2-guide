# Getting Started: First 30 Minutes

Subnautica 2 entered Early Access on **May 14, 2026** on Steam (PC & Xbox Series X|S) at $29.99.

**Story**: You are an Altera pioneer aboard the CICADA, bound for planet **Zezura**. The ship crashes. You wake from 14 years of cryosleep…

![Exploration Route](/images/exploration-route.svg)

---

::: tip Core Principle
**Three priorities**: Scanner > Habitat Builder > Tadpole. Survive → Build a base → Explore the ocean.
:::

## Step 1: Gather Basic Resources

The **Kelp Forest** around your Lifepod is your first resource zone.

| Resource | Source | Early Priority |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| Titanium | Metal salvage, seabed scrap | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Quartz | Coral domes (10-30m deep orange spheres) | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Copper | Cave below Lifepod (0-40m), limestone outcrops | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Salt | Common on seabed | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| Acidic Raion Pouch | Harvest/kill Raion creatures | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |

::: warning
The initial fabricator on your Lifepod can craft the **Survival Multitool** (harvest plants + mine minerals).
:::

---

## Step 2: Build Scanner + Standard Air Tank

- **Scanner** = Titanium × 2 + Quartz × 2 + Basic Battery × 1
- **Basic Battery** = Copper × 2 + Acidic Raion Pouch × 1
- **Standard Air Tank** = Titanium × 2 + Rubber × 1 + Silver × 2

The Scanner is the game's core — scan fragments and creatures to unlock blueprints and databank entries.

---

## Step 3: Build Fins + Flashlight

- **Basic Fins** = Rubber × 2 + Fiber × 2
- **Flashlight** = Titanium × 2 + Quartz × 1 + Basic Battery × 1

Rubber comes from **Lucifer Rotsac**, a glowing yellow plant. Find it ~84m from the Lifepod at heading 120°, depth ~12m.

---

## Step 4: Get the Habitat Builder

**Habitat Builder** = Titanium × 2 + Glass × 1 + Basic Battery × 1 + Copper Wire × 1

Scan **2 broken Habitat Builders** near Gene Donor (~160-225m from Lifepod) to unlock.

Once you have it, immediately build a **Corridor** (Titanium × 5) for:
- Oxygen refill point
- Storage (build lockers)
- Fabricator + Processor + Solar Panel

---

## Step 5: Unlock Genetic Adaptations

Visit the **tutorial Sampling Kiosk** and interact with the purple creature to unlock **Pressure Adaptation** — enables diving to **600m**.

Also find **Angel Comb** to unlock:
- **Digestion Adaptation** — safely eat alien food
- **Heat Tolerance** — survive in Hydrothermal Vents

---

## Step 6: Build Wakemaker + Tadpole

- **Wakemaker** = Silver × 1 + Grease × 1 + Basic Battery × 1 + Wiring Kit × 1
  - Scan outpost fragments to unlock
- **Tadpole** = Titanium Ingot × 2 + Glass × 1 + System Chip × 1 + Power Cell × 1
  - Scan 3 Tadpole fragments to unlock

The Tadpole requires a **Tadpole Dock** = Titanium Ingot × 2 + Silver Ingot × 1 + Copper Wire × 2.

---

## Step 7: What's Next?

1. Explore deep **Coral Gardens** for Silver and Gold (50-200m)
2. Enter **Zezuran Desert Hot Caves** for Lithium (200-250m, need Heat Tolerance + Tadpole)
3. Head to **Overgrown Ruins** for Celestine and advanced blueprints (200-400m)
4. Upgrade Tadpole depth modules → explore deeper zones

![Base Locations](/images/base-locations.svg)

::: danger Never Do This
- ❌ Enter Sparse Plains without a vehicle (Collector Leviathan territory)
- ❌ Enter deep caves without Beacons (easy to get lost and drown)
- ❌ Go to Hydrothermal Vents without Heat Tolerance (constant damage)
- ❌ Carry too many rare resources on exploration runs (lose everything on death)
:::

## Co-op Tips

Game supports **4-player online co-op**:
- Teammates can revive each other
- Resource gathering is shared (partial duplication)
- Leviathan aggro scales with player count
- Suggested roles: 1 base builder + 1 scout + 1 gatherer + 1 scanner
